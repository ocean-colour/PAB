#### What the Bokeh panel draws

The panel is built in `pab/viz/bokeh_argo_pace.py`, in
`ArgoPaceView.__init__`, `show_selection` and `show_profile`. The data
layer is `pab/viz/argo.py`. The panel is titled "BGC-Argo b_bp(700)":
b_bp(700) (m⁻¹, **linear**) against pressure (dbar, increasing downward).
Its layers:

| Layer | Style | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Raw BBP700 samples | grey dots (`#9e9e9e`) | re-fetched profile |
| De-spiked (3-point median over the BBP samples) | thin black line | `argo.bbp_layers()` |
| IQR-rejected samples in the mixed layer | red (`#c62828`) | `bbp_layers().dropped` |
| Argo ML mean ± std | blue line + 18 % band (`#1f77b4`), drawn from 0 to the MLD | DB (`mld_summary`) |
| "ML mean, sample de-spike" | blue dotted line, only when the re-computed mean differs from the stored one | `bbp_layers()` |
| MLD | horizontal black dashed line | DB |
| PACE (BING), NASA-GIOP | orange / green bands | **left out, as asked** |

- **Y range:** 0 to 2 × MLD (0–200 dbar when there is no MLD).
- **X range:** fitted to the samples in that window plus the bands.

#### I can reuse the existing data path, unchanged

- `bokeh_argo_pace.load_selection(store, wmo, cycle)` gives the stored
  MLD, the ML mean/std and the matchup dx/dt.
- `pab.viz.argo.fetch_argo_profile(wmo, cycle)` re-fetches the profile with
  argopy, exactly as the ingest did. This needs network access.
- `argo.bbp_layers(pres, bbp, mld, qc)` gives the raw samples, the
  de-spiked line and the rejected samples.

I tested this on **5906522 / cycle 79** (34.89°N, 140.63°E, off the Bōsō
Peninsula, 2024-07-22, dx = 1.1 km, dt = 0.7 h). The fetch took 7 s, with
65 BBP samples to 1,494 dbar. The re-computed ML mean (1.100 × 10⁻³ m⁻¹)
matches the stored one (1.091 × 10⁻³) to 1 %.

The figure will be a single function in `talks/AWOC2026/fig_argo.py`
(prompt 2), with two panels: the profile on the left and a zoomed map on
the right.
